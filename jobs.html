FULL TIME

Front-End Web Developer

Develop and maintain responsive, accessible, and user-friendly websites that support our clean energy projects. Work with designers and back-end developers to create reliable digital experiences across different devices and browsers.

Location

Melbourne, Australia

Salary

AUD $85,000-$105,000 / yr

Reports to

Digital Technology Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain responsive web pages using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Translate approved designs into functional and accessible user interfaces.
  • Collaborate with back-end developers to integrate APIs and dynamic content.
  • Test, debug, and resolve cross-browser compatibility issues.
  • Improve website performance, accessibility, and mobile usability.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong knowledge of HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
  • Understanding of responsive web design and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Familiarity with Git and version control workflows.
  • Basic knowledge of web accessibility standards and testing.
  • Good problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with a front-end framework such as React or Vue.
  • Familiarity with REST APIs and front-end performance optimisation.
  • Interest in renewable energy and sustainable technology.

REF: SOL02

FULL TIME

Backend Developer

Develop and maintain secure, reliable, and scalable server-side applications that support our clean energy projects. Work with databases, APIs, and front-end developers to deliver efficient digital solutions for customers and communities.

Location

Melbourne, Australia

Salary

AUD $90,000 - $110,000 / yr

Reports to

Backend Engineering Lead

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain server-side applications using Node.js or Python.
  • Design, develop, and maintain RESTful APIs for web applications.
  • Design and optimise database queries to ensure efficient data management.
  • Implement secure authentication, data validation, and error handling.
  • Collaborate with front-end developers to integrate server-side functionality.
  • Test, debug, and improve application performance, reliability, and scalability.

Essential Requirements

  • Proficiency in at least one server-side programming language, such as Node.js or Python.
  • Good understanding of relational databases, SQL, and database management.
  • Experience developing and integrating RESTful APIs.
  • Understanding of web security, authentication, and data validation.
  • Familiarity with Git and version control workflows.
  • Strong problem-solving, debugging, and teamwork skills.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.
  • Familiarity with Docker and containerised applications.
  • Knowledge of automated testing and continuous integration practices.
  • Interest in renewable energy and sustainable technology.
